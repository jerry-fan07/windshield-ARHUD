5.3 Safety rules for the whole build

The concave mirror concentrates sunlight

A 300 mm concave mirror at about f/1.1 is a burning glass. Never carry it, leave it, or point it with its face toward the sun, indoors near a window or outdoors. Store it face down on a soft cloth, or with its cover on. Once installed, the shutter is closed whenever the HUD is off; never defeat the shutter's spring or wedge it open for a test in daylight. Keep hands, cables and labels out of the focal region (about 330 mm in front of the mirror) when the shutter is open outdoors.

The bare backlight is very bright

Without the cell over it, the backlight runs at up to about {{l_bl}} cd/m², around sixteen times a clear sky. Do not look at it directly at full power; view it through welding glass, or bring it up at low current first. Point the luminance meter, not your eye.

Car electrics and driving

The fuse goes within 150 mm of the power source, before anything else. Never connect anything to the OBD-II port, the CAN bus or any vehicle wiring other than the accessory socket. Recordings are made from the passenger seat or by a second person; nobody operates the laptop while driving. Every drive starts with the signed installation checklist (Appendix F). Drives happen on the agreed closed course or lot, with a safety driver who is not the presenter, until the sign-off says otherwise. No polarised sunglasses while the HUD is on.

Workshop hazards

Heat-set insert tips and soldering irons are at 245–350 °C; use a stand and keep the cable clear. ASA prints emit styrene; run the printer enclosed with extraction or in a ventilated room, and do not sleep in the same room as a running ASA print. Wear eye protection when cutting the dust-cover glass, cleaving films, or crimping. The laser rangefinder is a visible laser; never aim it at people or cars. Lithium and lead-acid sources can deliver hundreds of amps into a short: insulate every joint before connecting to the car.

Static and the LCD cell

The panel's driver chips sit on the glass ledge and its flexible circuit is fragile. Work on the ESD mat with the wrist strap, never bend the flex near the glass, and never press on the polariser surface; a fingerprint on a polariser shows in the image at 23× magnification.

5.4 Records you keep

Keep a bound build log (or a text file in the repository) with the date, the stage, every measurement with its instrument, every decision with its reason, and every gate result. The appendices include blank sheets for the gates (Appendix E). Commit the calibration file, the dimming curve, the ray-trace inputs and outputs, and the CAD's regenerated files to the repository after each stage, so the build can be reproduced or repaired.
