6Materials: the complete bill

This chapter lists everything the build consumes, grouped by where it is used, with the build stage that first needs it. Prices are the plan's (last seen on 2026-10-21, not quotes); detailed items carry the plan cost line they fall under rather than a new price. The printed parts and fasteners are listed in full in B11, generated from the CAD.

6.1 The plan's cost lines

[[TBL cost]]

Optional, not in the total: a wedge-PVB windshield, installed ($600–1,500), only if the B13 ghost photographs call for it; an automotive HUD-grade TFT module ($200–800) if one can be bought. Already owned and not priced: the Jetson (reComputer J4012), two YODOIT 16-inch 1,200-nit monitors, two ACEMAGIC mini PCs.
