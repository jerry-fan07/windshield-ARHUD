No connector to the OBD-II port, the CAN bus, or any vehicle wiring other than the accessory socket(s).
Inline fuse fitted within 150 mm of the plug, correct rating, holder closed.
Nothing bonded, screwed or drilled into the car; nothing attached to the glass except the clamped road camera on the mirror stalk.
HUD housing restrained (pull test passed at installation) and at least 8 mm below the glass; not over an airbag door; not in the steering wheel's path.
All mounts removable without tools; all cables tied clear of the pedals, the steering column's movement and the seat rails.

GFormulas used in this guide

Virtual image of a lens or mirror (object at u inside f): 1/v = 1/f − 1/u, v negative (virtual); magnification M = |v| / u Plan's Stage 2: f = {{f2}}, u = {{u2}}, image at 7.5 m, M = {{m2}}, 60 × 30 mm crop becomes {{img2_w}} × {{img2_h}} Plan's Stage 1: f = 300 mm, u = 280 mm, v = −4.2 m, M = 15 Off-axis mirror at incidence i (Coddington): tangential radius Rt = 2f / cos i, sagittal Rs = 2f · cos i (both foci at f) Field of view for a window w at distance D: 2 · atan(w / 2D) Pinhole focal length in pixels for the virtual plane: (crop height in px / 2) / tan(vertical field / 2) Luminance at the eye: L_eye = L_panel × R_fold × R_mirror × R_windshield(s) × (cover, polariser, IR-cut, retarder transmissions) Plan path: 0.90 × 0.90 × {{rs65}} = {{path_ws}}; contrast ratio C = (L_overlay + L_background) / L_background ≥ 1.15 Backlight: L_backlight = L_panel / T_cell; flux Φ = π · L · A (Lambertian); LED power = Φ / (efficiency × lm/W) Fresnel reflectance (n = 1.52, 65°): s {{rs65}}, p {{rp65}} Ghost separation (plain laminate t, incidence θi, refraction θt): d = 2t · tan θt · cos θi; seen at distance D as d / D Laminate thickness from the laser double spot: t = d / (2 · tan θt · cos θi), sin θt = sin θi / 1.52 Curvature from a sagitta s under a straightedge L: R = L² / (8s) Age compensation: age = t_now − t_capture; s = ∫v dt, ψ = ∫ω dt over the age; p_now = Rz(−ψ) · (p_capture − [s, 0, 0]) Registration error from a range error Δz at range z, camera-to-eye baseline b: ≈ b · Δz / z² Parallax of an untracked head shift h for a point at distance d (image at 7.5 m): ≈ h · |1/7.5 − 1/d| (radians, h and d in metres) Half-wave retarder: output angle = 2 · (fast axis) − input angle

The parallax line uses the simulator's correction of the plan's table: the error is zero for a point at the image distance and grows for points nearer or farther, rather than following atan(h / d).

HGlossary

Age compensation
Moving each measured point by the car's own motion since the point's camera frame was captured, so it is drawn where it is now.
Backlight
The light source behind an LCD. Here, a custom LED box behind the 60 × 30 mm crop.
Cold mirror
A dielectric mirror that reflects visible light and transmits infrared. Used as the fold, it dumps solar infrared into an absorber.
Combiner
The partly reflecting glass that superimposes the image on the view: the windshield in Stage 2, a separate glass in Stages 0 and 1.
Conformal
Drawn so that it lies on the real object it describes, from the driver's eye.
Crop
The 60 × 30 mm (946 × 473 px) region of the panel that is lit and drawn; everything else stays black.
Eye box
The region the driver's eye can move in while still seeing the whole window: 130 × 70 mm by design.
Fold
The flat mirror that turns the beam so the optical path fits under the dash.
Gate
A pass/fail test (G0 to G7) that decides whether the next phase starts.
Ghost
The second, fainter copy of the image reflected from the windshield's other surface.
Hot mirror
A dielectric filter that reflects infrared and transmits visible light; in front of the cell it keeps focused solar infrared off it.
Lookup table (LUT)
The precomputed map, from the ray trace, from an eye position and a viewing direction to the panel pixel that appears there.
PGU
Picture generation unit: the panel with its backlight.
RANSAC
A fitting method that tries many minimal fits and keeps the one most points agree with, ignoring outliers.
Registration
How well a drawn mark lines up with its real object, as an angle at the eye.
Retarder (half-wave)
A film that rotates linear polarisation: output angle = 2 × fast axis − input angle.
s and p polarisation
Light polarised perpendicular (s) or parallel (p) to the plane of incidence. At the windshield, s is across the car and reflects far better.
Sagitta
The depth of a curve below a straight chord; gives the radius.
Sun-watch
The rule that closes the shutter when the computed sun direction enters the patch of sky the optics can focus onto the panel.
Toroid, freeform
A mirror surface with different curvatures in two directions (toroid), or a general polynomial surface (freeform), used to correct astigmatism and flatten the image.
Virtual image
An image that light only appears to come from; the eye can focus on it but it cannot be caught on a screen.
