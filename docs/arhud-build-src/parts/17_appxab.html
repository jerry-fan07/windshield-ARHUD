Conversions

NTC: R = 10 kΩ × n / (4095 − n) for ADC count n (the NTC is the lower leg); 1/T = 1/298.15 + ln(R / 10 kΩ) / B, T in kelvin, B ≈ 3950 (calibrate, B5.6) Driver level (inverted analogue input, 0.13 V full, about 4.2 V off): V = 4.2 − 4.07 × level; PWM duty = V / (3.3 × 1.33) MAX31855 frame (32 bits): D31–D18 thermocouple temperature, 0.25 °C per bit, signed; D16 fault; D15–D4 cold junction, 0.0625 °C per bit; D2, D1, D0: short to VCC, short to GND, open Servo: 20 ms period; pulse width between the measured closed and open end-points (about 1.0 and 2.0 ms)
