At a glance

Stage 1 is a magnifying glass. A 300 mm plastic Fresnel lens sits {{u}} mm above the panel, just inside its {{f}} mm focal length, so the eye sees an enlarged, upright image of the panel far behind it. A tilted sheet of glass, the combiner, reflects that image toward the driver while letting the road show through.

What this document adds to the plan
  1. Quote the image from the eye. The image is {{d_eye_img}} m from the eye, not 4.2 m, and spans {{fov_eye_w}}° × {{fov_eye_h}}° at {{ppd_mon_eye}} px per degree on the 4K monitor ({{ppd_55_eye}} on the 5.5-inch panel). The plan's figures are measured from the lens (section 3).
  2. The image is not flat. It curves away from the eye: {{isurf_c}} m at the centre, {{isurf_w_t}}–{{isurf_w_s}} m at the side edges. The eye cannot tell (0.06 D), but registration should use a measured distance per point, as Stage 2 does (section 4).
  3. Set the panel distance per lens, by parallax. A ±2% focal-length tolerance moves the image between {{tol_v_p2}} and {{tol_v_m2}} m. Put the panel on a slide and set it against a target (section 11).
  4. Grooves must face the eye. Flipped, only {{fw_f_100}} of the light at r = 100 mm passes cleanly (against {{fw_c_100}}), and the field edge shows {{flip_astig_D}} D of astigmatism (section 7).
  5. Per-colour pre-distortion. Red and blue separate by {{lc_edge}}° at the field edge ({{lc_edge_px}} pixels on the 5.5-inch panel); the split moves with the head (section 6).
  6. Stage 1 needs its own sun defence. It has no cold mirror, filter or shutter. Sunlight straight down the lens puts {{sun_peak}} kW/m² on the panel, and {{sun_worst}} kW/m² at {{sun_worst_ang}}° off axis. A lid that is closed whenever the HUD is off is the minimum (section 10).
  7. The path must be folded. 280 mm does not fit straight on a dash top; a 45° front-surface mirror makes a box about 410 × 116 mm (section 11; a suggestion, not in the plan).
  8. Crop and backlight disagree. Stage 1 uses an 80 × 40 mm crop; the custom backlight is designed for 60 × 30 mm. Either light {{bl_area_ratio}}× the area or accept {{fov60_w}}° × {{fov60_h}}° in Stage 1.
