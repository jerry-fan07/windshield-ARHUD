QuantityFormula and the values used
Image of a PGU pointMirror about the plane z = −x through the glass centre: (x, y, z) maps to (−z, y, −x). The PGU face z = −{{d_glass}} maps to x = {{d_glass}}, the road monitor's face.
Life-size field2 · atan(W / 2D) with W the monitor's active width and D the eye-to-image distance: {{hfov_shown}}° at {{d_img}} mm.
Window on the monitors2 · D · tan(fov / 2): {{win_w}} × {{win_h}} mm; divided by the pitch, {{win_px_w}} × {{win_px_h}} px; {{ppd}} px per degree.
Life-size crop of a source2 · f · tan(field / 2) source pixels across the shown field; the scale to the monitor is {{mon_px_w}} divided by that.
Coplanarity by parallaxSeparation on the far plane ≈ e · δ / D for an eye shift e and a plane offset δ: {{cop_px_per_mm}} px per mm at e = {{e_shift}} mm.
Parallax variantAngle per sway e: e · (1/D − 1/(D + Δ)). For the car's parallax between 7.5 m and z: k = 1/7.5 − 1/z; Δ = 1/(1/D − k) − D.
Contrast ratio and gainratio = (LHUD + Lbg) / Lbg; gain = (ratio − 1) · Lbench bg / LPGU white at the eye.
Fresnel at 45°, n = 1.52θt = {{theta_t45}}°; Rs = {{rs45}}%, Rp = {{rp45}}%.
Ghost separation2 · t · tan θt · cos θi = {{ghost_mm}} mm for t = {{glass_t}} mm; {{ghost_arcmin}}′ at {{d_img}} mm; relative brightness T² · R2 / R1.
Liftingα = atan((v − cy) / f); z = h / tan(θ + α); y = z · (u − cx) / f.
Age compensationpnow = Rz(−ψ) · (pcapture − [s, 0, 0]), s the integral of v and ψ the integral of ω over the age; tracks also by their own velocity.
Bench projection chainPGU pixel = HPR · F · S · Ksource · camera_from_car · pnow.
