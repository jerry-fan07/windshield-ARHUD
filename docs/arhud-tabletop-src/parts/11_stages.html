11.2 What the bench does not verify, so that no one thinks it did

  • Any optical property of Stage 1 or Stage 2: image distance, field, eye box, mirror figure, astigmatism, the windshield's ghost, polarisation at 65°, the solar focus.
  • Luminance at the eye in daylight, as an absolute: the bench reproduces ratios up to {{cr_sky_max}} against a background of {{l_road_white}} cd/m², not 10,000.
  • Stereo range and the per-frame plane fit; the eye-side range error of the plan's §5.4 (the camera-to-eye baseline is zero on the bench).
  • GNSS latency, the F9R, the IMU fusion, dead reckoning.
  • The controller, dimming, the shutter, the backlight, heat, vibration, the installation checklist.
  • The lane network's generalisation to the demo course: comma2k19 is one highway in California; the phone clips of T-C should be of the roads the demo will use.

11.3 Order and time

T-A and T-B are a few days each and independent; T-C needs the comma2k19 chunk downloaded (10 GB) and the MetaDrive exporter written; T-D is the long one, a week or two of network exports, scoring and the pitch tool; T-E and T-F are a day each once T-D holds; T-G needs the meter; T-H the webcam; T-I the camera and, for its full value, the Jetson. The plan's week-1 measurements (GPU time per job) come out of T-D on the Mac and are repeated on the Jetson under B2.
